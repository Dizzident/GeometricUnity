new, prospectively frozen functions of that already-determined dataset. It
does not advance a new Markov chain.

Hessian eigenvectors. The scalar edge space is decomposed into the exact

projector norms. It also represents the closed component as an `84 x 3`

alignment, its within-`C` squared Euclidean distance to the closed rank-one
valley, and the full-space squared distance obtained by adding the `C-perp`
