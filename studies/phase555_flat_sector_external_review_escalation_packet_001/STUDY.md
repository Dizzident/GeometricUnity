# Phase555 - Flat-Sector External-Review Escalation Packet

Amendment A30. Zero compute. This phase assembles the two reserved
measure-convention questions in a form a reviewer can answer, equipped with the
measured inputs from Phases550-552. It builds no mesh, evaluates no operator,
runs no linear algebra, and constructs no RNG.

## The two reserved questions

The standing firewall for the block is that measuring is permitted and
interpreting is not. Deciding that a measured null direction is redundancy of the
description, and normalizing by the corresponding transverse factor, is a
convention about the measure rather than a measurement. The register already
carries exactly those two items, and this phase adds none and modifies none:

- `O4-F1-COLLECTIVE-COORDINATE` - may the measured exactly flat 252-dimensional
  sector be classified as redundancy, so that a quotient by it is the correct
  construction for this registered action on this complete lattice?
- `O4-F1-FP-NORMALIZATION` - if it were, is normalizing by the corresponding
  transverse factor correct, given that the transverse curvature measured along a
  flat ray grows as the square of the ray parameter while 200 of the 252
  directions remain exactly flat there?

Both identifiers must already be present in the unchanged memo schema, which is
exact-bound and checked; the packet fails closed if either is absent.

## What the reviewer is handed

Only measured quantities: the threshold-free integer flat-sector dimension with
its topological split into 240 exact and 12 harmonic directions and its zero
operator contribution beyond the kernel of the discrete exterior derivative; the
per-configuration inertia and flat-block spectra at the six preserved pilot
positions; the exact homogeneous decomposition of the value there; the transverse
scale along the flat ray with its explicit model-based label; the measured rather
than declared observable invariance; the independent confirmation; and the
committed-chain drift reading.

Each question carries what each possible answer would and would not permit. In
every branch a separately preregistered construction under its own frozen
contract, its own controls, and its own independent adjudicator would be
required, and in no branch does a Phase458 gate follow.

## What it does not do

It authors no ruling, consumes no memo, verifies no signature, and changes no
pending flag; Phase480 semantics are unchanged. It applies no quotient, no gauge
fixing and no measure normalization, and reinterprets neither Phase548 nor
Phase549. External review remains pending and
`promotedPhysicalMassClaimCount = 0`.
