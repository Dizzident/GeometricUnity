## How the sources were used

The draft identifies a difference of connections and the transformed reference
connection. Phase582 derives a relative transport on a common edge, an adjoint
character invariant, and its local classical limit. The quaternion and complex
matrix controls, lift-sign tests, and conditional Haar pushforward are our
calculations; none is attributed to a missing theorem in the draft or to Wilson.

The source distinguishes a bare connection from its difference with another
connection. This matters because Phase581's bare-link decoy does not prohibit
a locally invariant statistic of a properly transforming pair. Both connections
must transform: holding the second fixed is not that symmetry.

The registered epsilon field uses SO(3) adjoint rotations. A fundamental SU(2)
trace alone depends on lift signs. Phase582 instead uses
q=(3-Tr_Ad(U_A U_B^-1))/4, which descends to SO(3). This loses center-sensitive
information by design, and does not establish suitability for the full GU
representation or physical fermions.

## Prediction relevance and limitations

This is a source-motivated, internally derived compact-link candidate. Its
classical small-spacing limit and conditional Haar density are controlled.
The actual registered A/B/omega/epsilon dictionary, action, quantum measure,
observer-space contraction, physical pole, and independent unit scale remain
missing. No source-contract field is filled and no particle mass is predicted.
The Haar premise is explicit, not silently substituted for the workbench's
Lebesgue coefficient measure. A large-q density enhancement exists already in
the free control and is not a condensate.
