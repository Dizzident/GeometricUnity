## Applicability question

The generic map is not accepted by name or comment. Phase556 checks whether
committed source supplies all of the bindings required to identify its
background, perturbation, endpoint/midpoint bracket, sign, and variable
semantics with the registered Phase548 action. A missing binding is preserved
as underdetermination. A direct contradiction is preserved as
inapplicability. Neither outcome may be repaired by tuning signs until a test
passes.

Only an exact source match permits the conditional origin arm. That arm first
verifies origin stationarity, then independently reconstructs the 240 exact and
12 harmonic directions, computes frozen exact/two-prime ranks and
intersections, and tests the origin infinitesimal identity. Even a successful
identity is reported only as algebraic evidence opening the separately frozen
Phase557 finite test. No direction is classified as redundancy and no
group-orbit or quotient statement is made.

## Executed result

The registered default-path positivity closure passed. Three deterministic
probes agreed bit-exactly with the independent squared-norm evaluation, and the
negative custom-weight decoy confirmed the boundary of that closure.

The transformation comparison found no committed bridge between the generic
map and the Phase548 operator. Four decisive terms were not source-bound: the
background-field identification, endpoint-averaging rule, theta
transformation, and registered action transformation law. The terminal is
`transformation-map-source-underdetermined`. The conditional origin arm
refused before rank or Hessian work, so Phase557 is closed; Phase558 must
adjudicate this negative branch independently.
