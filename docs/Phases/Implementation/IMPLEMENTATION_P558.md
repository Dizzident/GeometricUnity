# Implementation P558: Independent Transformation-Identity Adjudicator

Amendment A31. Deterministic, zero-sampling. Phase558 independently adjudicates
the applicable Phase556 or Phase557 branch and publishes an additive supplement
to the immutable Phase555 external-review packet. It is required on negative
as well as positive branches so a closed gate remains independently citable.

## Independence boundary

The project references neither Phase556 nor Phase557 and reuses none of their
implementation. Its preregistration contract exact-binds the applicable
artifacts and frozen source lineage. Known-answer sign, rank, Ward-term,
terminal-precedence, and negative-control batteries run before audited values
are consumed. The decisive applicability facts are reconstructed from the
bound sources rather than accepted from Phase556's booleans.

For the current branch, the adjudicator must distinguish an invalid input from
a source-map contradiction, a source-underdetermined map, an infinitesimal
identity mismatch, an absent finite law, or a finite closure/covariance
failure. It may report evidence ready for human review, but it cannot supply
either reserved human ruling.

## Review-packet supplement

The supplement is additive: it binds the immutable Phase555 packet, records
the independently checked machine evidence and its limitations, and does not
rewrite, replace, or reinterpret the original packet. In particular, a shared
origin coboundary or matching dimension is not elevated into a registered
transformation law. If the source bridge is absent, the supplement preserves
that negative result and identifies the missing bindings needed before a
finite-transformation falsifier can run.

## Executed result

The five-case known-answer battery passed before any Phase556 datum was read.
It includes two candidate background assignments that coincide at the origin
but differ off origin, a 240-plus-12 rank/intersection substitution decoy, the
full nonstationary Ward term, positive-form and indefinite controls, and every
terminal-precedence branch.

The independent raw-source reconstruction agrees with Phase556: the registered
default action path is nonnegative, but no bound source connects the generic
map to the registered action variables or transformation law. Phase557 is
absent behind its closed gate, and the conditional ranks and identity residuals
were not manufactured after that closure. The terminal is
`adjudication-confirms-transformation-map-source-underdetermined`.

The additive supplement was materialized with the byte-immutable Phase555
packet as its parent. It answers neither reserved ruling and records the next
unallocated task as locating or requesting a source-defined transformation law
with explicit field identifications.

## Authority boundary

Phase558 performs no RNG draw, HMC transition, chain replay, benchmark,
sampling, warmup, or adaptation. It consumes no protected Phase554 seed,
registers neither Phase553 nor Phase554, and does not apply a quotient, gauge
fixing, or measure normalization. It authors no ruling, changes no pending
flag, discharges no O4 item, satisfies no Phase458 gate, creates no Phase481
pack, selects no production default, and authorizes no production or launch.
It permits no physical-unit or GeV claim and preserves
`promotedPhysicalMassClaimCount = 0`.
