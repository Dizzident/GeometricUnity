# Implementation P555: Flat-Sector External-Review Escalation Packet

Amendment A30. Zero compute. Phase555 assembles the two reserved
measure-convention questions in a form a reviewer can answer, equipped with the
measured inputs from Phases550-552. It builds no mesh, evaluates no operator,
runs no linear algebra, and constructs no RNG.

## What is reserved, and why

The standing firewall for the A30 block is that measuring is permitted and
interpreting is not. Deciding that a measured null direction is redundancy of
the description, and normalizing by the corresponding transverse factor, is a
convention about the measure rather than a measurement. The register already
carries exactly those two items, and this phase adds none and modifies none:

- `O4-F1-COLLECTIVE-COORDINATE` - may the measured exactly flat sector be
  classified as redundancy, so that a quotient by it is the correct
  construction for this registered action on this complete lattice?
- `O4-F1-FP-NORMALIZATION` - if it were, is normalizing by the corresponding
  transverse factor the correct normalization, given the measured transverse
  behaviour along a flat ray?

Both identifiers must already be present in the unchanged memo schema, which is
exact-bound and checked; the packet fails closed if either is absent.

## What the reviewer is handed

Per question, the measured inputs and nothing more: the threshold-free integer
flat-sector dimension with its topological and operator decomposition, the
per-configuration inertia and flat-block spectra at the six preserved pilot
positions, the exact homogeneous decomposition of the value at those
configurations, the transverse scale along the flat ray with its explicit
model-based label, the measured rather than declared observable invariance, the
independent confirmation, and the committed-chain drift reading.

Each question also carries what each possible answer would and would not
permit - in every branch, a separately preregistered construction under its own
frozen contract, its own controls, and its own independent adjudicator, and in
no branch a Phase458 gate.

## What it does not do

It authors no ruling, consumes no memo, verifies no signature, and changes no
pending flag; Phase480 semantics are unchanged. It discharges nothing, applies
no quotient, no gauge fixing and no measure normalization, and reinterprets
neither Phase548 nor Phase549. External review remains pending and
`promotedPhysicalMassClaimCount = 0`.
